# Privacy policy

*Last updated: 27 September 2026*

FitGPX is designed to keep your activity data on your device.

- **No data collection.** FitGPX has no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking or crash reporting, and
  its developers receive no data from the app.
- **Your files stay on your device.** Files you open are read on your device, and GPX files are written only
  where you choose (a folder, a ZIP file, or an app you share to).
- **Map tiles.** When the map is shown, the app downloads map images from OpenStreetMap's tile servers.
  Like any website, those servers see your IP address and which map area is requested. See the
  [OSMF privacy policy](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy). You can turn the map off in
  Settings, and the app then makes no network requests at all.
- **Settings backup.** Your settings (including privacy zones) may be included in Android's device backup
  if you've enabled it. Converted files and caches are excluded.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/phil-tomlinson/fitgpx/issues.
