<p>Garmin, Wahoo, Coros, Hammerhead, Bryton, Zwift and most other fitness devices record activities as <b>.fit</b> files. <b>FitGPX</b> converts them to <b>GPX</b> on your phone, so you can use them in mapping, route-planning and analysis tools, without an account, a server, or uploading your location anywhere. It also syncs directly from a <b>UNA Watch</b> over Bluetooth, so you don't need a cable or another app to get your rides off it.</p>

<p><b>Standalone FIT → GPX converter</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Pick many files, a whole folder, a Garmin Connect export (.zip) or a Strava bulk export (.fit.gz)</li>
<li>Huge archives are streamed, so thousands of activities convert on a phone</li>
<li>Save to a folder you choose once, bundle everything into a ZIP, or share to any app</li>
<li>Open or share .fit files into FitGPX from your file manager, email or the Garmin Connect app</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Sync straight from a UNA Watch</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Pair once, then pull new recordings over Bluetooth with no cable and no separate companion app</li>
<li>Every file is verified against the watch's own checksum before it's kept</li>
<li>Only new recordings are copied; syncing again finds nothing to redo</li>
<li>Rides are named from their sub-sport, e.g. "Morning Mountain Bike Ride"</li>
<li>Once copied, they convert, trim and export exactly like any other .fit file</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Trim editor</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Drag handles on an elevation, speed or heart-rate profile to keep only the part you want</li>
<li>See the result on an OpenStreetMap map, and nudge the start and end point by point</li>
<li>"Trim idle" removes standing around before the start and after the finish</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Privacy</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Privacy zones remove your home or work from every export</li>
<li>Hide the first or last few hundred metres of any track</li>
<li>Optionally drop timestamps or heart rate before sharing</li>
<li>No accounts, analytics or ads. The map can be switched off for fully offline use</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Faithful output</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Elevation, time, heart rate, cadence, power and temperature in the GPX dialect read by Strava, Garmin Connect, Komoot, Ride with GPS, OsmAnd and GoldenCheetah</li>
<li>Course points (turns, climbs, water) become waypoints</li>
<li>Recovers GPS data from files left truncated by a crash or flat battery</li>
<li>Multisport activities become one track per sport; optional GPS-spike removal, simplification and pause splitting</li>
</ul>
